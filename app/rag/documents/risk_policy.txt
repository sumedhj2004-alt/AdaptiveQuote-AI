FINANCIAL TRANSACTION RISK POLICY

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines the basic rules used to identify and review potentially risky financial transactions.

2. TRANSACTION RISK LEVELS

LOW RISK

Transactions with normal behavioral patterns and no significant anomaly indicators may be classified as low risk.

Low-risk transactions generally require no manual review unless another business rule is triggered.

MEDIUM RISK

Transactions showing unusual behavioral characteristics may be classified as medium risk.

Examples include:
- Moderate deviation from the customer's normal transaction amount.
- Unusual transaction location.
- Unusual device usage.
- Increased transaction frequency.
- Transaction activity outside the customer's normal operating hours.

Medium-risk transactions should be monitored and may require additional verification.

HIGH RISK

Transactions showing strong indicators of unusual or potentially fraudulent behavior may be classified as high risk.

Examples include:
- Transaction amount significantly higher than the customer's normal transaction amount.
- Large change compared with the previous transaction.
- Transaction activity during unusual hours.
- Transaction from an unusual location.
- Transaction from an unfamiliar device.
- Multiple unusual behavioral indicators occurring together.

High-risk transactions should be sent for human review before approval.

3. HUMAN REVIEW

A high-risk transaction should be reviewed by an authorized financial risk analyst.

The analyst should examine:
- Customer transaction history.
- Transaction amount.
- Previous transaction behavior.
- Location.
- Device information.
- Transaction timing.
- Applicable business policies.

4. MODEL OUTPUT

The machine learning model provides a risk signal and should support, not replace, appropriate human review and business controls.

Model predictions should be evaluated periodically using new transaction and review data.

5. FEEDBACK

Human review decisions should be recorded.

Examples:
- Confirmed suspicious transaction.
- Legitimate transaction.
- Requires additional investigation.

Review feedback may be used in future model evaluation and retraining.
